Your repo and how to publish

Your repo is github.com/vibe-jam-2026/team-9 and what you build is published at vibe-jam-2026.github.io/team-9. You are reading this page there now: what you build replaces it. These instructions stay at vibe-jam-2026.github.io/team-9/instructions.html.

How to publish

  1. Open claude.ai/code in the browser and log in with team9@rawe.se. Adrian gives you the login code in the room. Accept the invitation to VibeJam2026 when it shows.
  2. One person in the team clicks Sign in with GitHub and approves with their own GitHub account. That person must have accepted the invitation to the team's repo, or Claude cannot push. Then pick the repo vibe-jam-2026/team-9.
  3. The team shares one Claude login. Agree on who steers and when. Do not change its password or settings.
  4. Your page starts from index.html at the top of the repo. Everything the browser needs must be in the repo as static files: HTML, JavaScript, CSS, images and sound.
  5. Claude pushes its work to a branch of its own. Publish by asking it to open a pull request, then click Merge on github.com, so the change lands in main.
  6. GitHub Pages publishes every change to main by itself. About a minute later the new version is live at your address.
  7. Built with a tool such as Vite? Put the finished files at the top of the repo or in docs/. docs/ is picked up by itself when there is no index.html at the top. Use relative paths, because your site lives under /team-9/.
